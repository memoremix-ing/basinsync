# Basin

A tenancy domain and *Reservoir*[^][reservoir] containing multiple
*Flows*[^][flow]. Its data-state *Timeline*[^][timeline] is the
*watershed*[^][watershed]; other *Timelines*[^][timeline] can serve separate concerns.
